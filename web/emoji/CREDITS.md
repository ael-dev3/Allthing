# Emoji credits

Allthing's Pepe pack: emotes of Pepe the Frog that their makers shared publicly on 7TV, FrankerFaceZ and emoji.gg, for anyone to use in chat. Pepe the Frog was created by Matt Furie. Allthing serves its own copies (`v1/`, WebP, 64 px tall; each animated one has a still, `.still.webp`, for readers who ask for less motion) so a cast's emoji never load from those sites.

The images in `v1/` are not covered by Allthing's AGPL license: each belongs to whoever made it, credited below with where it came from. They were chosen as public emotes with no stated restriction: on 7TV, listed emotes with no flags; on FrankerFaceZ, public, approved emotes; on emoji.gg, only those under its "Do whatever you want with this emoji" license (its default license, which allows only platforms that don't charge for access, was skipped). Some animations were re-encoded smaller, at most 150 KB, and a few at half their frame rate.

If you made one of these and would rather it were credited differently, or not here, please open an issue.
