# Emoji credits

Allthing's Pepe pack: emotes of Pepe the Frog that their makers shared publicly on 7TV, FrankerFaceZ and emoji.gg, for anyone to use in chat. Pepe the Frog was created by Matt Furie. Allthing serves its own copies (`v1/`, WebP, 64 px tall; each animated one has a still, `.still.webp`, for readers who ask for less motion) so a cast's emoji never load from those sites.

## Terms

The images in `v1/` are not covered by Allthing's AGPL license: each belongs to whoever made it, credited below with where it came from. Each one's **Terms** say why it may be here, as checked on 20 October 2026:

- **7TV public**: listed on 7TV, with no flags. 7TV states no license for its emotes; their makers share them there for anyone's chat.
- **FFZ public**: public and approved on FrankerFaceZ, which states no license for its emotes either.
- **emoji.gg free**: under emoji.gg's "Do whatever you want with this emoji, have fun!" license.

Anything with a stated restriction was left out, among them emoji.gg's default license, which allows only platforms that don't charge for access. Some animations were re-encoded smaller, at most 150 KB, and some at half their frame rate.

If you made one of these and would rather it were credited differently, or not here, please open an issue: it goes from the next build.
