# Changelog

All notable changes to Allthing are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html) (0.x while it is a prototype; see [docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md)).

## [Unreleased]

- Feed refreshes retain all three levels of already loaded quotes. They no longer reread known nested quotes or collapse them to links when the provider is unavailable or the request budget is tight.

- Keyed Solana RPC failures keep the private endpoint and provider content out of public wallet error responses, while preserving status, timeout and request-budget diagnostics.

### Added

- Animated Pepe stickers. A sticker button beside the emoji button opens 32 larger Pepes (128 px GIFs from 7TV, credited in `web/stickers/CREDITS.md`) in the picker's Stickers tab, your recently used ones first, searched as the emoji are. A sticker goes with the cast in the GIF's place, as an image embed of its GIF at allthing.social, so every Farcaster app shows it. Allthing shows its stickers large and frameless, apart from a cast's photos, and as stills with reduced motion.
- Each app's daily active accounts, on the front page's client bar. Pointing at an app's segment or name (tapping it on a phone, or resting keyboard focus on it) opens its card: how many accounts were active through it in the 24 hours to the last whole hour, its share of the trending casts, and a link that shows only its casts. An account's app is the one that requested the key it signed with, so an account that used two apps counts for each. The cron keeps each hour's signing keys and learns each key's app once, from the event stream for keys added onchain and otherwise by looking up the account's keys, 24 accounts a run. Once an hour D1 counts the past 24 hours' keys by app, exactly. After a deploy, the first count by app comes once 24 hours of keys are kept, and until every account's apps are known (about 10 hours of lookups) a card says what share is, and gives its count as a floor. `/api/v1/activity` serves the counts as `apps_24h`.
- Farcaster's daily active accounts, counted as SnapMeter counts them: an account is active when one of its messages (a cast, reaction, follow, profile change and so on, on SnapMeter's closed list) was merged into the network, and it counts once in a window. A cron trigger every 2 minutes reads Hypersnap's event stream on from where it stopped and keeps each UTC hour's and day's active accounts as compact sets in D1, so the counts are exact. `/api/v1/activity` serves the 24 hours to the last whole hour and the 24 before, as SnapMeter's rolling window `(t − 24 h, t]`, the 30 days to yesterday, and each day's count with its messages by kind, saying when counting began and any gaps. On 20 October 2026 Farcaster had 6,174 active accounts in 24.5 hours. The client usage bar and a page of their own will show them next.
- An emoji picker in the composer. A Pepe beside the GIF button opens the Pepe pack: a grid that searches by name and description as you type (Enter picks the first), with your recently used emoji first (kept in this browser). Each emoji is still until pointed at or focused, and the foot of the picker shows the one pointed at with its shortcode, beside a link to the credits. A pick puts its `:pepe_name:` in your text at the caret, spaced from the words beside it. A click closes the picker (Shift keeps it open), and on a phone a tap leaves it open for another, without the keyboard. While a GIF or emoji picker is open, the text box shrinks to two lines so both fit.
- Pepe emoji in casts. `:pepe_name:`, in any case, shows one of 103 Pepe emoji, 82 of them animated: Pepe holding up a NO sign (`:pepe_no:`), smug, blushing, hugging a heart, sitting, and many more. They are public emotes from 7TV, FrankerFaceZ and emoji.gg, each credited with its source and maker in `web/emoji/CREDITS.md`, and Allthing serves its own copies as WebP (64 px tall, at most 150 KB each), loaded lazily. A cast of one to six emoji and nothing else shows them large; with reduced motion they stand still. A shortcode that isn't in the pack stays text, and other Farcaster apps show the shortcode as written.
- Following, unfollowing and deleting one's own casts from Allthing, with the key a reader approved for liking. On anyone else's profile, Follow sits beside the name once Hypersnap has said whether the reader follows the account (one read from the browser per profile view); hovering Following offers Unfollow, and the follower count follows along. Under the reader's own post and replies, Delete asks once more in place ("Delete this cast?", Keep focused), then takes the cast back; it reads "You deleted this cast." wherever it shows in that tab, after a reload too, since cached pages may carry it for a while. Each is signed in the browser and handed to Hypersnap, as a like is; the encodings were checked against Hypersnap, which refused them only for the throwaway key's signer.
- Casting, replying and quoting from Allthing, with the key a reader approved for liking and recasting. The composer's Cast, Reply or Quote (new, beside Reply under a post and its replies) posts from the browser: "@name"s that belong to someone become mentions, looked up at Hypersnap; the GIF and the quoted cast are embedded; a reply answers its cast and a cast on a channel page goes to that channel. Over 320 bytes it is a long cast, up to 1,024 bytes, counted as the text nears it. A cast that goes out closes the composer with a notice that links to it; one the network refuses keeps the draft and says why. A reader with no key yet approves one first. The composer offers no way round to the Farcaster app; only where the Worker endorses no keys does the draft open there instead.
- Liking and recasting from Allthing, for a signed-in reader. The first like or recast opens a dialog with a QR code to scan with the phone's camera and approve in the Farcaster app, which adds an app key to the reader's account. The key is made by the reader's browser (WebCrypto Ed25519, not exportable) and kept there (IndexedDB); Allthing's servers never see it. The Worker only endorses it: a `SignedKeyRequest` signed with the custody key of Allthing's own Farcaster account (`/api/v1/auth/signer`), which the Farcaster app shows as Allthing's request; the signature was checked against the validator's own `validate()` on OP Mainnet. From then on, the heart and recast icons under every cast like and recast, and undo it: the browser signs each message and hands it to Hypersnap. The button and count change at once and change back, with a notice, if the network refuses; a key removed in the Farcaster app is forgotten, and the next like asks for a new one. Casts the reader liked or recasted show it, from their latest 100 of each and, on a post page, that cast. Signing out, or "Stop liking from this browser" in the account menu, deletes the key. It needs the `ALLTHING_FID` and `ALLTHING_CUSTODY_KEY` Worker secrets (`ALLTHING_SPONSOR=1` pays readers' approval fees from the account's warps; docs/DEPLOY.md); without them a signed-in reader's heart and recast icons are pictures, and `/api/v1/status` says which (`writes`).
- Writing a cast or a reply, with a GIF, for a signed-in reader. Cast, at the top right of the front page's feed and of a channel's, starts a cast, in the channel on a channel page: a pill in Allthing's indigo accent turning to the portal cyan, lettered in the wordmark's serif, and on a phone a round button floating at the bottom right of those feeds, with notices above it; Reply under a post or a reply starts a reply. Where Allthing doesn't post it with the reader's key (above), Post in Farcaster opens the draft in the Farcaster app (farcaster.xyz's compose link), where the reader posts it. The GIF button opens a picker of KLIPY's trending GIFs, with a "Search KLIPY" box that searches once typing pauses; the browser asks KLIPY directly, as KLIPY requires, with a key the Worker hands signed-in readers (`/api/v1/gifs/key`). It needs the `KLIPY_API_KEY` Worker secret (docs/DEPLOY.md); without it there is no GIF button. `/api/v1/status` says whether it is set (`gifs.keyed`).
- Signing in recovers by itself. A code that runs out while the dialog is open is replaced, twice, before the dialog offers a new one. When the Worker can't check an approval just now (Optimism can't be read), the same approval is checked again after 1, 2 and 4 seconds and then on "Try again", with no new code to scan. A hidden tab stops asking Farcaster's relay and asks at once when it is back in view. The dialog shows who approved while the Worker checks, the signed message lapses with the sign-in's nonce on the Worker's clock, and signing in or out in one tab shows in the others. Adapted from Warpkeep's sign-in.
- Sign in with Farcaster, by QR code: "Sign in" in the header opens a dialog with a QR code, and nothing else to choose, to scan with the phone's camera; the person approves in the Farcaster app. The Worker checks the signed message as Farcaster's auth client does (the nonce in a cookie bound to the browser, this site's domain, the signature's signer, and on OP Mainnet that the signer is the account's custody or auth address), then keeps the session in an HMAC-signed, HttpOnly, Secure cookie for 30 days. The header shows the account's picture: a click opens their profile, and hovering it (keyboard focus resting on it, or a tap on a touch screen) shows a menu with their profile and "Sign out". It is the only way to sign in, it posts nothing, and it needs the `SESSION_SECRET` Worker secret (docs/DEPLOY.md). The static copy has no sign-in.
- A speed report: `e2e/speed-report.spec.ts`, run by CI's `speed report` job on a manual run, loads the front page of allthing.social cold seven times at 1280 px (10 Mbps) and at 390 px (slow 4G, CPU slowed 4×), and prints the medians of time to first byte, WebAssembly and data arrival, first cast, LCP and the slowest interaction. See docs/PERFORMANCE.md.
- Names in a Liked by or Recasted by list open their profile card beside the list on hover, keyboard focus or tap, as names elsewhere do; the list stays open, and a tap opens the card instead of the profile.
- `/api/v1/status` says whether Solana wallet counts have a keyed RPC endpoint (`solana_rpc.keyed`, and the endpoint's host, never its key), so a deploy can be checked after setting the `SOLANA_RPC_URL` secret.
- The usage bar filters the feed: each app is a link to its casts, the filter is a removable chip, and "Other apps" lists the smaller clients. The filter stays in the URL with the sort and time range.
- API answers carry a `Server-Timing` header with the Worker's time for them, named after how they were served (`fresh`, `revalidating`, `miss` or `stale-if-error`), so slow page builds can be measured in production from any browser.
- Production performance checks wait for the intended main commit to deploy and fail clearly if it does not arrive, instead of benchmarking the previous release.

### Changed

- The heart and recast icons under a cast never open the Liked by or Recasted by list. They like and recast; for a reader signed out, a click opens the sign-in dialog, and once they have signed in, and approved a key if they had none, the like or recast goes out. A sign-in dialog closed without signing in drops it. Pointing at an icon or its count previews who, as before; the preview's "See everyone", or a click on the count, opens the full list. On a phone, a tap on the count, which has a target of its own beside the icon's, previews who.
- A profile card opened from a Liked by, Recasted by or Replies preview is complete from the frame it shows. Once the pointer or keyboard focus comes into the preview's list (on a touch screen, once a tap opens it), the vote and wallet activity of the people it names load, in the list's order, two at a time and at most 20 people per 5 minutes. A card whose vote or wallets are still on their way waits for them out of sight, for half a second at most, instead of showing them loading and growing when they come.
- A profile, feed or post that isn't cached yet builds faster: it checks at most six unknown links for mini apps per build, in one parallel batch, instead of twelve in two batches. The second batch waited for the first's slowest page; on cold profiles it took 1.2 to 1.8 s of 1.6 to 2.6 s. Links left unchecked keep their casts listed until the next refresh checks them.
- A profile that isn't cached yet builds faster again: its quoted casts and app names are read while its links are checked for mini apps, instead of after the check. On 19 October 2026 the check took up to 1.4 s of an uncached profile's build, and the reads after it another 0.1 to 0.2 s. What only a hidden cast needed (its quotes, its origin) is left out of the payload, as before.
- Links to block explorers (Etherscan, Basescan, Arbiscan, Polygonscan, Celoscan, BscScan, Uniscan, Blockscout, Solscan and Solana Explorer) are no longer read to check for mini apps: their pages never declare one. A single Basescan link held an uncached profile's build for the whole 2.5 s link-page timeout.
- A link page read to check for a mini app is read only up to the end of its `<head>`, where the meta tags are, instead of up to 128 KB: a large page from a slow server spent much of the 2.5 s the read is allowed on a body that is never looked at.
- Nothing on screen calls a post or an account spam. A hidden reply says "Post hidden", a hidden quoted cast "Quoted cast hidden", and a filtered post opened by its link says which kinds of links Allthing leaves out. What is left out is unchanged; mini app posts still say they are mini apps.
- Solana wallet counts are real when the `SOLANA_RPC_URL` secret is set: each verified Solana address is paged through its history (up to 5,000 signatures, shown as "5,000+"), from a keyed RPC endpoint. Without the secret, a Solana read that fails says it couldn't be read just now, instead of blaming the public RPC. See the Secrets section of docs/DEPLOY.md.
- A quote of a quote shows inline, up to three levels: a quoted cast that quotes another shows that quote as a card, and so on. A quote beyond the third is a link, as before. Each level costs one bulk read in a listing's build, made while the build has budget to spare.
- The Wallets button on a profile opens its list of verified addresses on hover or keyboard focus, on screens that hover, as the other popovers do. A tap or click still toggles it.
- Wallet activity by chain sits in the profile header, under the joined date and NS vote, instead of a sidebar card. It is the same chart and Wallets list, at the same width as the rest of the profile on phones.
- Opening a post, a profile or a channel directly (from a link elsewhere, or a reload) starts its data request as soon as the page arrives, alongside the app's download, as the front page already did. Before, it waited for the app to boot, about 450 ms on production. It is still one API call.
- A profile opened from a feed shows its header at once: the picture, names and fid from the casts the page showed, and the start of the bio, the follower counts and the join date from the profile card its payload carried. The casts load behind it, as a post's replies do. Where nothing was known about the account's bio and counts, they show placeholders, and a join date the profile would correct (an account from before November 2023) waits for the profile. Before, the page stayed a grey placeholder until the whole profile arrived, which took seconds when it wasn't cached.
- Profile-card vote and wallet prefetches resume when foreground reads finish or fail, so navigating with pending profile facts no longer leaves the next feed's cards waiting for another interaction.
- Sort tabs answer at once, and scrolling stays smooth as cards are added. A list rendered anew (a sort tab, another page) draws a screenful of cards in the task that asked for it and the rest a screenful at a time after each paint; a re-sort starts again from the top, and infinite scroll adds its 25 cards the same way. Before, the 25 cards were built inside the click (13–39 ms of WebAssembly on a fast desktop CPU). Back and forward still draw the whole list at once while they restore the reader's place.
- The app's WebAssembly downloads a tenth smaller again: 393 KB instead of 441 KB. Its views are built with Leptos's type-erased components (`--cfg erase_components`), which boxes each reactive closure instead of generating view code for each. That costs 3–6 ms more to draw the first cards on a fast CPU, and saves about 240 ms of download on a slow 4G link.
- The app's WebAssembly downloads a fifth smaller: about 434 KB instead of about 550 KB. The build compresses it at brotli's highest level, and the Worker serves that copy at `/wasm/` to browsers that accept brotli; the edge's own on-the-fly compression is faster but larger. If the Worker can't answer (its daily allowance spent, say), the page loads the static copy as before.

### Removed

- The temporary per-stage marks in API answers' `Server-Timing` header, added to measure uncached profile builds, are gone now that the measuring is done. The header still carries the Worker's total time and how the answer was served.
- Profiles and profile cards no longer show the Farcaster app's spam label for an account ("Labeled likely spam", "Not labeled spam", "Nerfed", "no label") or the note that came with it. The label shards the build compacted from that app's public dataset are gone too, with the `cargo xtask labels` task, and builds no longer download the 125 MB dataset. Leaving out spam by a cast's content is unchanged.

### Fixed

- A profile card that opens before its vote and wallets arrive (after the half second it waits for them, from a Liked by, Recasted by or Replies preview, say) no longer grows when they do: it grew by 27 px for an account with two chains, and by 115 px for one with five chains and a note. The vote row is always there ("Unknown" when the vote can't be read), and the wallet section keeps one height whatever comes: the bar with one line of legend (two chains by name and "+N" for the rest), or one message, with any note folded into a word beside the "Wallet activity" label ("partial" or "earlier counts") that explains itself on hover and to screen readers. Profiles keep the full legend and notes.
- The sign-in dialog no longer says Allthing can't post or change anything for the reader, which stopped being the whole story once it could like, recast and post. It says that signing in tells Allthing who they are, nothing more, and that liking, recasting and posting take a key they approve once in the Farcaster app.
- The production performance check also accepts a deployment of a later commit that includes the pushed one. Workers Builds skips a commit that a newer push supersedes, so after a docs-only merge (which runs no CI) the pushed commit itself could never go live, and the check would wait out its 30 minutes and fail.
- The production performance check waits up to 30 minutes for the pushed commit to go live, not five. Workers Builds takes 6 to 16 minutes, and about 25 when two merges queue, so since the wait was added the check failed on every push to `main` without measuring anything.
- A profile card opened from a Liked by or Recasted by list shows the person's bio, follower and following counts and join date, like cards elsewhere, instead of "The bio and follower counts are on the profile". The lists carry them, from the user objects that come with the reactions: no extra request, and the preloaded lists bring them too.
- In the Replies preview, each reply's author is a link to their profile, and hovering, focusing or tapping it opens their profile card beside the preview, as names in Liked by do, with the bio, counts and join date that come with the replies. The reply's own text still opens the reply.
- A reply with no text no longer shows as a blank gap in the Replies preview: its image thumbnails load at once (they were left to lazy loading, which left them blank there), and a video, link or quoted cast is named in a few words.
- A profile card opened from a name in a Liked by, Recasted by or Replies list sits level with the list, the way the list opened, instead of hanging from the name: lower names put it past the list and the post it belongs to. On a phone, with no room beside the list, it still opens by the name. Its wallet notes have room between them.
- A profile card's picture no longer flashes blank as the card opens: until its sharper copy has loaded, it shows the small copy the feed already loaded. A post's author and a profile's own picture do the same, and load at once instead of lazily.
- Wallet activity no longer swings when a chain can't be read: that chain keeps its last good count from the previous read of the same addresses, and the chart says some counts are from an earlier read. Before, it was left out and the other chains shared its part (0xael.eth showed Optimism 58% and Monad 41% while Base, its main chain, was unread), and that copy was kept for six hours. A copy still missing an EVM chain is now read again within minutes.
- Wallets distinguish unread chains from measured zero activity, and keep the earlier-read notice when carried counts are zero.
- Search and the channel directory's search no longer show a channel count that contradicts the channel's page: Hypersnap's channel search can return a different record of the same channel, so /farcaster showed "23 casters" in search while its page shows 445,633 followers. Search rows now show the channel without a count.
- A post's meta line names the app it was posted from once ("via Farcaster"), not twice: the feed card's "via" chip had stayed next to the post's own linked one since the app moved into the meta line.
- Taps, clicks and keystrokes show their result sooner on every page: about 16–32 ms instead of 40–64 ms after the input in a headless browser without a GPU. The line of light drifting along the header animated its background, which repainted the header in every frame while it ran (always), and the next frame after an input waited behind that work. It now slides by transform, which the compositor animates without repainting; it looks the same and still stops under reduced motion.
- The changelog's 0.4.0 entries are under their headings: sixteen fixes had landed above them, and four additions were filed as fixes. Two missing entries are back (the sound badge's quick second tap, and cards outside the window skipping layout), and the Solana entry says that allthing.social couldn't read Solana.
- Wallet activity by chain follows the current rules even when the edge serves a payload it cached under older ones, which it keeps for hours. For up to a day after 0.4.0, a chain under 1% could still show as 1% (0xael.eth's Optimism, 0.3% of its transactions). The browser now works out the shares from the counts itself.
- Wallet activity says when Solana isn't counted. Solana's public RPC refuses Allthing's requests (from browsers, and so far from Cloudflare Workers), so an account's Solana wallets were left out of the chart without a word; an account whose only wallets are on Solana read "No onchain activity". The chart now says "Solana isn't counted: its public RPC refuses Allthing's requests", and the wallet payload's `sources` no longer claims Solana was read.
- Older cached wallet responses also redact private Solana RPC diagnostics without losing counts, carried flags or cache policy.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added

- Past month, Past year and All time are real, distinct ranges, ranked from Allthing's own index of notable casts in Cloudflare D1. A cron job reads one page of casts a minute: the past week's trending casts, and channels' and notable authors' casts further back in time. It keeps the casts whose points could rank, and every few minutes ranks each range's top 1,000 inside D1. The front page reads one ranking row and the page's casts, fresh, in one bulk call, and says how many casts the range was ranked from and how far back they go. Until a range can be ranked, it shows the past week's trending casts and says so. The indexer keeps to half of D1's free daily limits, and `/api/v1/status` shows its progress. The static copy has no index and leaves these ranges out.
- Video sound sticks: once the reader turns a video's sound on (its badge, its controls, or a click to play it), later videos autoplay with sound until they mute one. The choice is kept in the browser's local storage. The "Sound off · tap for sound" badge on an autoplaying video is now a button that turns its sound on or off in place. Where the browser won't play sound before the reader has interacted with the page, the video plays muted and its sound comes on at their first tap or key press.
- Profile cards: hovering a profile picture or name, or resting keyboard focus on it, shows a small card about the account: its picture and names, the start of its bio, followers and following, when it joined, its Farcaster vote, the Farcaster app's label and its wallet activity by chain, with a link to the profile. They are on names in feeds, quoted casts, posts and replies, the full list of likes and recasts, people found by search and a channel's hosts. On a touch screen a tap shows the card, and its "View profile" link opens the profile; a mouse click still opens the profile directly. The bio, counts and join date come with the page's payload, from the accounts its casts arrive with, at no extra request; the vote and the wallets are read when a card opens, and a card never asks for the whole profile.
- Spam is left out of every feed, search and reply list, like mini apps: casts linking to `*.vercel.app` apps, anything mentioning or linking gig-bot, and DEGEN reward or airdrop bait ("Just unlocked my DEGEN rewards", `?degen=1` links). A spam reply that has replies of its own stays as "Spam post hidden" so the thread still reads.
- The Farcaster app is called Farcaster: casts posted from it say "via Farcaster", not "via Warpcast" (its account, fid 9152, still has its old name). The front page shows a thin bar of the apps today's trending casts (or those of the time range picked) were posted from, with each app's share, from each cast's provenance (the app that registered its signing key). It comes with the trending payload, so it costs no extra request, and shows once at least 20 casts have a known app.
- Channel pages show the channel's banner, picture, description, follower and member counts, link, lead and moderators, and its pinned cast above the feed, from the Farcaster app's public channel API. Member counts are now true member counts: Hypersnap's number counted distinct casters (it said /dev had 15,664 members; it has 475), so where only that number is known, as in search and the static copy, it is shown as casters. Where that API can't be reached, the page shows what Hypersnap gives.
- The header search box suggests as you type: once typing pauses for a quarter of a second, a list of up to 4 people (picture, name and handle), 3 channels and 3 posts drops down, from the same cached search the results page uses. Arrow keys move through it and Enter opens the highlighted item; a click opens one; Enter with nothing highlighted, or the last row, opens all results; Escape closes it. The box is an ARIA combobox with a listbox of suggestions.

### Changed

- The Farcaster vote is called the NS vote (Network School), with the NS icon beside it and a tooltip that explains it, on profiles and hover cards. The answers are unchanged: Voted yes, Voted no or Didn't vote.
- Wallet activity charts leave out chains under 1%, so each row shows a whole percent and a chain at 100% is the only row. An EVM chain that couldn't be read is named as missing instead of silently dropped from the shares. `docs/WALLETS.md` records the sources, the method and the explorer cross-checks.
- Like and recast popovers on feed cards open with their people already listed, without "Loading…": the cards in view load them ahead, six cards per request (`/api/v1/engagement`, edge-cached; one at a time, at most four per page view, and none on touch screens, with Save-Data or on 2g, or in the static copy). A replies popover reuses the post that hovering its card prefetched. Who liked or recasted a cast is now listed newest first across Hypersnap's shards, not just within the last one.
- Profile cards open after 100 ms instead of 350 ms, and complete: the vote and wallet activity of the authors of feed cards in view are prefetched (two at a time, at most 12 accounts per 5 minutes, only where a pointer hovers), so an opened card has no loading state.
- Profiles' wallet activity includes Solana: the transactions involving the account's verified Solana addresses (up to 1,000 each, from Solana's public RPC) count alongside EVM chains, so the chart reads like "Base 44% · Solana 55% · other". The static copy still can't read Solana, whose RPC refuses browsers. On allthing.social, though, Solana's public RPC refuses requests from Cloudflare Workers, so profiles there say Solana wasn't measured.
- Post pages drop the "Posted via" box. The posting app is a quiet line in the post's meta ("via Farcaster"), and its signing key and app fid show on hover.
- Profiles say when the account joined. Accounts registered before the ID registry moved to OP Mainnet in November 2023 showed "before Nov 2023", the earliest date onchain data has for them; they now show when they got their first username (dwr: 17 Jun 2021), read from the fname server's public transfer log, which the old sign-up registered along with the account. If that read fails, the profile keeps the date its previous copy had and is rebuilt within a minute. Newer accounts show their onchain registration, as before.
- The front page has a Reddit-style time filter next to hot and top: All time, Past year, Past month, Past week, Today (the default) and Past hour, kept in the URL as `t`. Up to a week, it picks the window Hypersnap ranks trending casts over: 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days. Past month, Past year and All time are ranked from Allthing's own index (see Added). A link with a time range still costs one API request, started by the inline script.
- Pull requests run deterministic browser regressions against the local build, alongside the Rust checks. Browser failure reports are retained for 7 days; live production performance checks keep their separate job.
- Feeds show each cast's whole text, with its paragraphs, line breaks, links and mentions, instead of its first 120 characters as a one-line title. A cast with only media shows just its media, without an "[image]" or "[video]" placeholder.
- The front page's data request starts from a small inline script in `index.html` as soon as the page arrives, alongside the WebAssembly download, instead of after the app starts. It is still one API call.
- Media is bigger. A single image or video takes its own shape, as wide as the card and at most 70% of the window tall (80% when portrait), instead of at most 580 px wide and 460 px tall. Its frame fits it, so a narrow image has no bars beside it, and a portrait video is no longer letterboxed in a 16:10 box. Back to a feed still lays images out at their size at once, and now videos too.
- Pages no longer say "Updated N min ago". The notice that a cached copy is shown because the data source isn't answering stays.
- Feeds show a quoted cast inline, as a small card with its author, text and media, instead of a "↳ quotes a cast" chip. The quoted casts come in the same payload as the feed (one bulk read per page build), so the card is there at once. A click on it opens the quoted cast. A quoted cast that is spam or a mini app says so instead of showing its content, there and on post pages.
- Search results stay compact: the first 6 people and 8 channels, laid out in columns with a count on each group, and "Show all" to see the rest. Casts start above the fold, and their text no longer takes the group headings' display font.
- The header's Channels button and the front page's Channels sidebar are gone: the feed is one centered column, and the Worker no longer looks up trending channels for it. Channels are still found through search and channel links, and `/channels` still lists them.
- The README is rewritten, with a wordmark for light and dark themes, CI, license and release badges, the features, the architecture and a quick start. A code of conduct, issue forms for bugs and feature requests and a pull request template join refreshed contributing and security guides, stale screenshots are gone, and `.gitignore` covers what local wrangler and Playwright runs leave behind.
- Cards outside the window skip layout and painting until they come near it (`content-visibility: auto`), so re-sorting a long feed lays out only what is on screen. A card with an open popover is always drawn whole, so the popover isn't clipped.

### Removed

- Neynar scores are gone: no score badges beside names in feeds, posts, replies, popovers and search, and no Neynar score on profiles. The Worker no longer reads scores from Neynar's contract on Base, which saves up to 2 subrequests per page build.

### Fixed

- Frame-like metadata inside fallback raw-text elements, including `noframes`, no longer hides an ordinary linked post. Actual metadata following the fallback element is still detected.
- A rate limit or unreadable upstream page no longer marks an indexed backfill feed as finished. The indexer keeps its cursor and retries it on a later turn; genuinely missing feeds still finish.
- Video sound controls use native focus behavior. Browser tests activate already visible controls without automatically scrolling them first, preserving the viewport while checking sound, focus and keyboard activation.
- Queued video events now respect the element's current playback state. An old pause event cannot hide sound controls after playback resumes, and an old playing event cannot show them for a paused video.
- The static copy reads linked-page metadata only up to its byte limit and cancels the remaining body. A large or stalled tail no longer delays a usable head or requires buffering the full response. CI also checks the static payload builder.
- If the app module or WebAssembly fails to load, the loading glow gives way to an accessible error message and Reload button. Reload preserves the requested page and query.
- Quoted casts have their linked pages checked for mini-app metadata, sharing the build's twelve-link quota with ordinary casts. Locally deferred checks remain eligible on the next build, and carried quote verdicts survive feed refreshes.
- The browser response cache now enforces its 200-entry capacity even when all answers are fresh. Static text uses the same bound, with expired entries removed before the oldest remaining answer.
- Wrangler's development image decoder uses Sharp 0.35.5, which includes the patched librsvg for GHSA-wq5f-xc86-pv6w. An override keeps the existing Wrangler and Miniflare versions; browser CI rejects high-severity npm advisories.
- Playing an autoplaying HLS video reuses its buffered source when it is already the best rendition the stream can supply. Low-resolution videos no longer restart the same rendition to satisfy an unavailable higher resolution; genuine upgrades still work.
- Leaving or replacing an MSE video cancels its rendition playlists and segments, pending source/buffer event waits, and idle buffering timer. Removed players no longer keep those operations alive.
- Ordinary linked pages are no longer mistaken for mini apps because a comment, script, template, or quoted attribute contains a metadata example. Actual frame and mini-app declarations in the page head still apply.
- The newest feed keeps stale-if-error recovery when both event shards are unavailable, rejects error objects as event pages, and accepts genuinely empty streams. Invalid event timestamps are skipped without overflow.
- Changing the front-page time range starts that range's pagination immediately. Requests and retry timers from the previous range cannot block it, hide its loading state, or append an obsolete page.
- Editing the header search immediately hides suggestions for the previous words, and Escape also dismisses a lookup that is still loading. Arrow keys and Enter cannot open a result from an older query.
- An API answer with valid JSON but missing or invalid payload fields is requested again on the next visit. Malformed prefetched payloads are discarded instead of keeping the page in an error state for five minutes.
- The "Sound off · tap for sound" badge does what it says: it acts on the state it shows and changes its label at once, so a quick second tap no longer acts on a state the reader no longer sees.
- The Farcaster app is no longer called @warpcast anywhere: the "via" tooltip says "requested by Farcaster (app fid 9152)", and the "via Farcaster" links go to the app's account by fid instead of its old @warpcast name.
- On post pages, the likes, recasts and replies popovers no longer show under the replies or under a native tooltip: the post lifts above the replies while a popover is open, and the counts row no longer carries a `title` tooltip (its ranking note is read to screen readers instead).
- DEGEN staking promotions are left out as spam, like DEGEN reward bait: casts linking to or naming yeetr.fun, and "stake your DEGEN" or "yield on your DEGEN" pitches. Casts that just discuss staking stay.
- "new" on the front page lists the newest casts on Farcaster, seconds to a minute old. It used to re-sort the trending pool, whose newest cast was hours old: Hypersnap ranks trending casts with a lag by design (even its 1-hour window's casts are 1–2 hours old), and Hypersnap's search index was about 8 hours behind. "new" now comes from Snapchain's event stream (`/api/v1/latest`): each build reads only the events since the previous one, adds the new top-level casts and drops removed ones, and the list is rebuilt at the edge every 30 seconds. `/status` reports how old the newest cast in the new and trending lists is, and how old each list is.
- Muted text (times, handles, counts, the "read-only" pill) is easier to read: its color now has at least 4.5:1 contrast on cards and on the page background (it was 4.0:1 on cards and 3.7:1 on the background). Post pages have a level-one heading for screen readers ("Cast by …"), and the posting app's link in "Posted via" is underlined instead of told apart by color alone.
- The static test server answers malformed paths with 400 instead of crashing, and rejects paths into sibling directories whose names share its root prefix.
- Copying a verified wallet waits for the clipboard write before showing success, and reports failures with a retry instead of falsely saying “Copied”.
- Profiles preserve unknown vote states when the leave-vote source is unavailable or records disagree, and identify app-reported revocations without claiming they were confirmed onchain.
- Reaction dialogs keep keyboard focus inside, restore it when dismissed, and lock background scrolling. Their profile links use the app router, and navigating or going Back closes the dialog.
- Profiles say how an account voted in only three ways: "Voted yes" (stay), "Voted no" (leave) or "Didn't vote". A stay vote that Network School's moderation revoked counts as not voting, since revoking cancels it on Base. When the sources can't settle it, the vote is left out instead of saying "Unknown", and while it loads an empty pill replaces "checking…".
- Card text can be selected: dragging across it selects it instead of dragging a link, and doesn't open the post, while a plain click still does. The post's link for keyboards and screen readers is the timestamp.
- The YouTube and Vimeo click-to-load thumbnail no longer enlarges or brightens on hover. Media isn't highlighted or enlarged on hover anywhere.
- Clicking a card outside its links opened the post only after about 4 seconds. It used a view transition whose promise waited for an animation frame, which never comes during a transition, so Chrome's timeout ended it. Cards now open posts directly, in under 100 ms, with a short CSS fade-in.
- The docs and the stylesheet no longer mention or use view transitions, which cards stopped using in 0.3.x; the leftover cross-document `@view-transition` rule is gone too.
- The changelog's 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 entries link to their release tags (`v0.1.0`, `v0.2.0`) and compare views instead of bare commits. `scripts/check.sh` checks every release links to its tag.
- Revisiting a page after an API error (a timeout, a 500, "busy") asks the API again instead of showing the same error for up to 5 minutes. Prefetches no longer store errors, and a garbled answer is never reused as data.
- Back to the front page deep in the feed returns to the same card and stays there. Before, the list came back shorter (25 cards, without the later pages), and a position saved by pixels could not be reached or was moved by images loading, so it could jump to the top a few seconds later. The place is now saved as the card at the top of the viewport, lists come back as long as they were, and the place is held for 6 s unless the reader scrolls.
- Back to a feed no longer moves the page after the reader's place has been restored. Images above that place came back at zero height and grew as they loaded, pushing the page by hundreds of pixels at a time, sometimes after the 6 s hold had ended. Images seen before are now drawn at their size straight away.
- Going Back or navigating to another page dismisses the media viewer and releases its scroll lock, instead of leaving it over the destination page. Closing with Escape still returns focus to the image that opened it.
- A video about to autoplay no longer flashes the play icon over its poster: the icon stays hidden from the moment it is due to autoplay until it plays. It comes back if playback hasn't started within 2.5 s, for example when autoplay is refused.
- A video autoplaying muted no longer has the play button drawn over it, so it no longer looks paused. A small "Sound off · tap for sound" badge shows instead, and a click anywhere plays it with sound. Videos are muted and inline in their markup, and muted again before every autoplay, as iOS Safari requires.
- A video the reader started keeps playing when another video on the page could autoplay. Before, a muted autoplay that started at the same moment took over and stopped the reader's video.
- Autoplay goes to the topmost video in view. On phones, where a post's video and a reply's can both be on screen, whichever loaded last used to take over, so the reply's video could stop the post's. Once the playing video scrolls away, the next one in view starts.
- The Content Security Policy allows Cloudflare Web Analytics' script (`static.cloudflareinsights.com`). Cloudflare injects it when Web Analytics is on, and before, the browser blocked it on every page and logged an error.
- A cast whose posting app couldn't be looked up (Hypersnap failed, or the request's budget ran out) is looked up again on a later view. Before, it was remembered as having no app for as long as the Worker stayed warm.
- The header search box says "Search", so its placeholder no longer gets cut off on phones. Its accessible name still says what it searches.
- When the next page of the front page fails to load, it is tried again after 2, 4 and 8 seconds. Before, one failed page ended the feed for the rest of the visit.
- A short or empty filtered page no longer stops infinite scrolling while later casts remain. Lists recheck their end after a page arrives, respect retry backoff and the 1,000-cast request limit, and release their observer callbacks when unmounted. Ranked front-page batches are cached between reactive reads.
- Changing the front-page sort reorders each loaded trending page, including later pages, while keeping page boundaries stable as more arrives. Later pages retain their posting-app metadata, including when returning to the feed. A request that finishes after navigating away no longer accesses disposed feed state.
- Popovers (who liked, recasted, replies) open toward the side with more room, so the replies preview on the first card no longer slides under the sticky header. Their height fits the space available, and the card with an open popover sits above the next card instead of under it.
- Popovers (who liked, recasted, replied, and the Wallets list) have a solid background. In Chrome, a photo in the card above could show through them: their see-through, blurred background was drawn beneath images on their own compositing layer.
- Timestamps with impossible dates (30 February, 31 April), out-of-range time zone offsets or trailing text are rejected instead of being rolled over into a different date.
- Channel parent URLs and search terms retain literal percent escapes instead of being decoded again. The static API parser treats form-encoded spaces and literal plus signs the same way as the Worker.
- On touch screens, tapping a likes, recasts or replies count opens its popover. Before, the tap's focus opened it and the tap itself closed it again. On narrow screens, popovers are nudged to stay on screen.
- Escape closes a keyboard popover without reopening it on the returning focus. Preview links stay in the app, and leaving an open preview removes its outside-click listener.
- On phones, a cast with a wide image no longer makes its list wider than the screen.
- A cast whose channel URL holds a non-ASCII character where an address would start no longer crashes the page that lists it.
- On phones, profiles show the wallet activity panel and its Wallets button right after the profile header, instead of below every cast. The Wallets popover opens toward the side with more room, like the others.
- Profiles open by any name the account holds. `/u/dwr.eth` said "No such user": Hypersnap looks names up by each account's current fname only, and fid 3 is now `dwr`. Other names, ENS names among them, are now looked up by the hub's username proof, which names the account's FID.
- Casts that embed mini apps hosted on their own domains (for example a `*.vercel.app` app) are left out of every feed, search and reply list, like those on Farcaster's hosts. Hypersnap gives no embed metadata, so the Worker reads the linked page's `fc:miniapp`, `fc:frame` or `of:` meta tags: a few links per build, with each verdict remembered per URL.
- Clicking play on a video while its playlist is still loading plays it, with sound, once it has loaded. Before, the click was lost and the video stayed still.
- Leaving a loading video no longer lets a late playlist or autoplay timeout access disposed state and panic the app. Autoplay timers are cleared when replaced or when the video is removed.
## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added

- **Search**: one box in the header for casts, people and channels, with results grouped. Search hits are re-read in bulk so they carry their media and counts.
- **Who liked, recasted and replied**:
  - Hovering (or focusing) a likes or recasts count shows the ten newest people. Clicking opens the full list (up to 100, as many as Hypersnap returns).
  - Hovering a replies count shows a scrollable preview of the most engaged replies, with avatars, scores, text and image thumbnails, and a link to the full thread. On touch screens a tap opens it.
- **Wallets popover** on profiles: every verified EVM and Solana address, each with one-click copy and a "Copied" confirmation.
- **The Farcaster vote** on profiles: whether the account voted to stay or to leave when farcaster.xyz asked, or did not vote.
  - Stay votes are read onchain on Base, from Network School's opt-in NFT keyed by FID.
  - Leave votes come from farcaster.xyz's public API, since they are not recorded onchain.
  - See [docs/VOTE.md](docs/VOTE.md).
- **Instant navigation** ([docs/PERFORMANCE.md](docs/PERFORMANCE.md)):
  - A post opens from the cast data already in the feed and loads its replies behind it.
  - Hovering or touching a card prefetches its post, and cards that stay in view are prefetched in idle time, within a capped budget.
  - Infinite scroll loads the next trending page before the reader reaches the end.
  - Navigation uses View Transitions and Speculation Rules, with preconnects to media hosts.
  - Back and forward restore the scroll position.
  - CI checks a performance budget against the live site.
- **Videos autoplay muted when scrolled into view** and pause when scrolled away, one at a time. They never autoplay with reduced motion or Save-Data.
- The whole post card is clickable, while text stays selectable and links inside it still work.
- **Release process**: Semantic Versioning from the workspace `Cargo.toml`, Conventional Commits, this changelog, tagged GitHub releases, and short-lived branches merged by squash ([docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md)).
- The version and short commit appear in the footer, and the Worker answers `/version` (also `/api/v1/version`).
- A test of Neynar scores for known FIDs, and [docs/SCORE.md](https://github.com/ael-dev3/Allthing/blob/v0.3.0/docs/SCORE.md) on why the score is read onchain.

### Changed

- **Casts that embed mini apps or frames are filtered out server-side**: from the front page, channels, profiles, search and reply previews. They are detected by URL (`farcaster.xyz/miniapps/…`, `farcaster.xyz/~/mini-apps…`, `warpcast.com/~/frames…`, `*.neynar.app`, including links in the text), or by `fc:frame`/`fc:miniapp` metadata where an API provides it. In threads, a mini app reply is dropped, unless it has replies of its own; then it stays as "Mini app post hidden" so the thread holds together.
- Profiles keep only the wallet activity infographic in the side area.
- The header has Home, Channels and search; the footer is one line (license, source, version).
- Changing the sort reorders the list in place instead of rebuilding the page, and expanding replies keeps the scroll position.
- Neynar scores are cached for 12 hours instead of 6 (they change weekly at most).
- GitHub Pages now redirects to https://allthing.social, keeping paths and queries.

### Removed

- The "Hypersnap" backend badges on casts, the "Read from" and ingress lines of the post's provenance box, and "view on farcaster.xyz" links.
- The keyboard shortcuts box and the j/k/o/x bindings, with their selection highlight. The media viewer keeps its arrow keys and Escape.
- The About page, the intro box, "How ranking works", "Where data comes from", and the "Front page" heading.
- Hover enlargement of images.
- On profiles: the verified addresses list, the "Posts via" breakdown, the per-chain "no activity" notes, the Solana note and "How this is measured".

### Fixed

- Clicking play on a video that was autoplaying muted could leave it muted.
- Search queries with characters that Hypersnap's query parser treats as syntax (`:`, quotes, brackets) no longer fail.
