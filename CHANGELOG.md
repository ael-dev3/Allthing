# Changelog

All notable changes to Allthing are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html) (0.x while it is a prototype; see [docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md)).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.5.1] - 2026-10-23

### Changed

- **For you and Following.** Signed-in readers have two front-page tabs, each with hot, new and top and every time range. For you lifts casts from accounts you follow; Following shows their casts only. New stays chronological, including with Favorite and Show less.
- **Seen posts.** Later front-page visits leave out casts you read for a second in a visible tab, including cards taller than the screen. Show seen posts brings them back, and Reset seen posts clears the history in Settings. History stays in this browser, separate for each reader: at most 2,000 hashes for 30 days, across four readers. Cards stay put while you read; direct posts, profiles and threads stay complete.
- **Mini apps in Settings.** Show mini app casts is in the header's Settings panel for every reader, replacing the feed's hidden-cast count and switch. Only casts and quotes that contain mini apps show their Open in Farcaster links, and quoted mini apps follow the setting immediately.
- **A clearer header.** Phone search has a full-width second row, keeping Home, the online count, Settings and account controls in view. The online count reserves its space on desktop and stays beside Home on phones.
- **Verified Messages.** Messages verifies the signed-in Farcaster account before it can chat or send messages. The Worker's attestation key is pinned in the SpacetimeDB module, which rejects forged and expired attestations.

### Fixed

- Future-dated casts cannot outrank the present in For you or Following, including All time and early paint. Cached newest-cast payloads and index rankings exclude future timestamps before selecting their visible rows. Ingest retains up to five seconds of source clock skew in the existing buffer; implausible dates are discarded, and retained rows appear only when their timestamp is reached.
- A directly opened mini app post follows Show mini app casts immediately in both directions, without a reload or refetch. Hiding it removes its body and embedded content while retaining its Open in Farcaster link; ordinary posts keep their content.

- Short time ranges include newly posted casts even while Hypersnap's trending list lags behind. Every sort keeps the chosen range, and the end-of-range note appears below the list without moving cards down.
- Following prefetches its own feed when changing sort or range, stops automatic paging at the time limit, and resumes from that cursor for a wider range. A fully seen or filtered feed offers Load more posts after ten payloads rather than reading indefinitely.
- Changing accounts replaces the feed and ranking snapshot, and late answers from the previous account cannot refill its cache. Presence no longer counts an anonymous and signed-in visit twice; hiding or signing out also removes the public name from cached lists.
- Messages connects under the existing Content Security Policy without permitting JavaScript evaluation. Pressed conversation rows keep their previews and timestamps readable.
- Notifications coalesce refreshes from opening the page and regaining focus. Marking the list read no longer queues a duplicate request that could start after the tab is hidden.
- A wrapped reply's app link clears the collapse button's full tap area.

### Development

- Cold-load performance checks retain sanitized per-sample LCP, network, cache and long-task diagnostics, including on failure. Their timing limits, cold contexts and sample count are unchanged.
- Browser regressions wait for restored seen rows and the latest presence-cache expiry, including a deliberately delayed count refresh, while preserving their exact ordering, privacy and timing checks.
- Local macOS builds find worker-build's cached WebAssembly optimizer. SDK wire-format and strict-CSP browser checks cover Messages; feed-ranking, reply-composer and smoothness fixtures exercise the updated UI without changing their budgets.
- The attestation-key helper replaces the complete Rust key array, including multiline values, so a generated key compiles.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- A signed-in reader's front page ranks the casts of the people they follow. Hot and top on `/` read `/api/v1/following` (the session's account's follows, from Hypersnap's `feed/following`, cached per account and never for another reader) and rank them as before, with Show less and favorites; a **Following / Everyone** menu beside the sort tabs switches to the trending feed and its time ranges (`?feed=everyone`). Where they follow few who cast lately, up to 40 trending casts follow theirs, and a note says so. Signed out, nothing changes; early paint skips a signed-in reader's front page.

- Show mini app casts: a switch in the account menu, over any feed that has mini app casts ("3 mini app casts hidden"), and on a mini app post. On, feeds, channels, profiles and threads show casts with mini apps or frames, each with a "Mini app · Open in Farcaster" link; Allthing still doesn't run them. Off is the default. The choice is kept in this browser and followed by its other tabs. The Worker now marks mini app casts instead of dropping them (one payload for everyone, no new requests), and the index keeps them; spam is left out as before, and search, reply previews and notifications still leave out both.

- @mention suggestions in the composer, for casts, replies and quotes: typing "@name" lists matching people under the caret, those you follow first and marked "Following"; arrows move, Enter or Tab picks, Escape closes.

- A reply's page shows the casts it answers as full cards, root first, each avatar joined to the next by a line, in place of the dashed "In reply to" snippet. A chain of more than three folds its middle behind "Show N earlier", which opens in place. The cards keep their media, counts, actions and hover cards, and hidden casts are left out as elsewhere.

- The encryption for end-to-end encrypted messages, as pure, tested Rust (`allthing_core::e2e`). After the browsers' X25519 agreement, it uses HKDF-SHA256, with both parties' identities, key versions and public keys in its info, and then XChaCha20-Poly1305, with a random 24-byte nonce, over the text padded to 32 bytes. The associated data binds the sender, the recipient and their key versions and keys. The tests pin a key and a ciphertext built on RFC 7748's keys, which `tests/e2e-vectors.test.mjs` recomputes with Node's own crypto.

- Verified Farcaster accounts on SpacetimeDB. Signed in, a browser's SpacetimeDB identity gets the Worker's word that it is your account (`POST /api/v1/auth/attest`, an Ed25519 signature by a key of its own, the new `ATTEST_KEY` secret, valid 10 minutes), and the module's new `verify_fid` reducer checks it against the key's public half pinned in the module and marks the account verified. Once the key is pinned, only verified accounts can chat or send messages there. `node scripts/attest-key.mjs | npx wrangler secret put ATTEST_KEY` makes the key; `/api/v1/status` says `spacetime.attest` and the public half (never the key). A test runs it on the real Worker.

- The SpacetimeDB module behind Messages (the one inbox and its end-to-end encrypted messages) is in the repository (`spacetime/`), as published to maincloud as `allthing`, with its checks in CI: formatting, Clippy, unit tests of what it accepts, the wasm32 build and the license audit (`sh spacetime/check.sh`).

- Protocol test vectors for the messages Allthing signs (`crates/allthing-core/tests/message_vectors.rs`). The encoder is checked against Snapchain's own test vectors (two casts from its engine tests, signed by its test key), the example like in Snapchain's HTTP API reference, and five messages on mainnet as Hypersnap served them: a like, a recast, an unlike, a quote and a deleted cast. Each is rebuilt from what the node says it holds and must hash to the very hash its signer signed, and each signature is verified against its signer's key (`ed25519-dalek`, a test-only dependency: Allthing signs with the browser's WebCrypto).

- The composer's bar is as compact as the Farcaster app's, and it posts with Cmd or Ctrl and Enter. Its tools are 32 px icons on the left (attach, emoji, sticker, GIF, the clock; a 40 px tap on a phone), and on the right a ring shows how long the text is, as in the Farcaster app: it fills toward a cast's 320 bytes, then toward a long cast's 1,024, turns amber in the last 75 bytes and gives way to the bytes left under 25, red and below zero past the limit (a screen reader hears it only as it crosses those lines). Cmd or Ctrl and Enter in the text box posts, but not in the GIF search, where Enter searches; a reply's button reads "Replying…" while it goes. Signed in, a post page fetches the composer's code and the GIF key once it is idle, so the first Reply opens with its bar, with no "Loading…"; a press on a touch screen starts the fetch too.

- A reply shows what it answers, as the Farcaster app's composer does. Above the text box, which now says "Cast your reply": the author's picture, name, @username and how long ago, the first five lines of the cast (three on a phone) with its Pepes, its first four images and videos as squares of a fixed size (96 px, 64 on a phone; a video with its poster and a play sign), and "Replying to @username", with a line from the author's picture down to yours beside the box. It is drawn from the cast on the page, so opening it asks for nothing, the box has focus by the next frame, and nothing moves as its pictures load. Screen readers hear one line with the box: "Replying to @alice: " and the start of the cast. A scheduled reply you change shows its line as before.

- Notifications in the app, for a signed-in reader. A bell beside your picture in the header counts what's new ("9+" past nine), from the first page of your notifications, read when the app starts, every 3 minutes while the page is in view, and when you come back to it. `/notifications` lists them grouped as the Farcaster app groups them, under the tabs All, Mentions (mentions, and quotes that mention you) and Replies: each row has its kind's icon, up to five faces, what happened ("**alice** and 5 others liked your cast"), the cast's start with its Pepe, and when. A row opens its cast, or for follows the newest follower's profile, and a face or a name opens that person's profile card. What came since your last visit is tinted for this visit, and opening the page marks it all seen, so the bell clears, in your other tabs too. Load more goes further back. On a phone each face, name and tab takes a 44 px tap. Your browser keeps what you've seen, by account; the static copy has neither the bell nor the page. The page's code comes as the bell is pointed at or touched (24.9 KB at brotli 11), and the module grows by 5.6 KB at brotli 11 (494,256 → 499,893 bytes).

- Notifications, from the Worker: `/api/v1/notifications` gives a signed-in reader who liked, recasted, replied to, mentioned and quoted their casts, and who followed them, a page at a time, grouped as the Farcaster app groups them ("alice and 5 others liked your cast", "alice and 3 others followed you"). Hypersnap's notifications list a recast as one more like, and a mention or a quote as a reply: the hub's newest recasts of the casts liked tell recasts from likes, each cast tells replies from mentions and quotes, and the quotes of your newest casts, which Hypersnap doesn't list, are read on their own. Replies, mentions and quotes that are spam or mini apps are left out, as feeds leave them out. It answers only a session, for the session's own account, is cached at the edge for a minute per account, and no browser keeps it. What you've seen is kept by your browser, not the server.

- Images and videos in the composer. In a new cast, a reply or a quote, the attach button (on a phone, your photos), a pasted screenshot or files dropped on the composer attach PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP images and MP4, MOV and WebM videos. Each uploads at once, with its progress and Remove, which stops it; one that doesn't go says why, with Retry. The cast embeds them in order, two embeds at most with its GIF, sticker or the cast it quotes, and the buttons say when there's no room. A photo's location, camera and other metadata are taken out in your browser before it's uploaded (JPEG, PNG and WebP; GIFs and videos go as they are). Handed to the Farcaster app, the draft carries them; scheduled, it keeps them. Where a server has no R2 bucket bound, the composer has no attach button and takes no files.

- Every button, tab, chip and link answers a press at once, as an app's do: in the frame after a finger, the mouse or Space or Enter presses it, it shrinks a little and dims, and it eases back over 90 ms when let go. Nothing around it moves. A link in a line of text only dims, and so does everything with reduced motion. On a phone the browser showed no press at all (Chrome shows `:active` only once a touch has lasted a moment, Safari only on pages that listen to touches), and the Cast button's and the heart's eased in, so their first frame showed nothing; the smoothness check now finds all 22 controls it presses, on the front page, in the composer, on a post and on a profile, answering in their first frame at 390 and 1280 px. A tap on a control is no longer taken for half of a double-tap zoom.

- Image and video attachments, the Worker's part. `POST /api/v1/media` takes a signed-in reader's PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP image (up to 10 MB) or MP4, MOV or WebM video (up to 50 MB), sent from Allthing itself, and keeps it in R2; `https://allthing.social/m/<fid>/<id>.<ext>`, the URL a cast embeds, serves it to anyone, in any Farcaster app: in ranges (as Safari's video player asks), with a year's caching, only ever as the type it was uploaded as, with `nosniff` and a sandbox policy, so a file that isn't what it says can't run as a page. The Worker hands the upload's body to R2 as it comes and never reads it, so an upload takes the same CPU time at any size: the Worker's WebAssembly memory stayed at 1.31 MiB through two 45 MB uploads. A reader may upload 300 MB and 60 files a day, 20 a minute; everyone together, 2,000 a day; and the files kept stay under 9 GiB, within R2's free 10 GB-month, counted in D1's new `media` table. `GET /api/v1/media` tells the composer whether it may attach; its button comes next. Until R2 is enabled and the bucket bound (`docs/DEPLOY.md#attachments-r2`, nothing else needed: no plan change, public bucket or token), uploads and `/m/` answer 404 and `/api/v1/status` says `media.ready: false`.

- Smoothness budgets in CI (`e2e/smoothness.fixture.spec.ts`, docs/PERFORMANCE.md#smoothness). On fixture data, a signed-in reader at 390 px (touch, the CPU slowed 2×) and at 1280 px taps the sort tabs, opens a likes count, likes, opens the composer, opens a cast and a profile from three places in the feed and goes Back, goes Back deep in the feed, scrolls, and presses nine controls, each three times. The median must answer within 100 ms (INP), with no long task overlapping it, no blank or white frame in a navigation, nothing moving after a navigation's first frame (layout shift < 0.01), the reader's card back within 2 px, and every press shown in its first frame. What the app doesn't meet yet runs as an expected failure with what it measured: no press shows in its first frame, every navigation has 2 to 6 frames with nothing to read, and opening a cast or a profile on the phone takes 136 to 232 ms, most of it drawing the page in the tap's own task.

- The logo as a PNG, for the picture of Allthing's Farcaster account, @allthingapp: https://allthing.social/pfp.png, since Farcaster apps show an SVG picture such as `favicon.svg` as blank. It is the favicon's serif "A" on its ground, 512×512 and opaque, with the "A" well inside the circle apps crop a picture to. It is cached for a year, so a new logo gets a new file name.

The caret, selections, Backspace, the arrow keys, Enter, paste, copy and cut work as in a text box: Backspace takes a Pepe away whole; a paste is plain text, its shortcodes shown as Pepes; a copy gives the shortcodes. The tap that opens it brings the keyboard up at once on iOS too, with the text box focused.

- Allthing's poll on the front page: "Would you like an Allthing mobile client?", Yes or No. On a wide screen it sits beside the feed and stays in view as you scroll; on a phone it is a card after the fourth cast. It moves nothing on the page. Anyone can vote, signed in or not. A vote shows how many voted each way at once ("Yes 63% (5)", how many votes, and yours), and this browser keeps it, so a later visit shows the results without asking; before a vote there are no numbers. One vote counts per network (an IPv4 address, or an IPv6 address's /64), so people who share an address share a vote, and a network that has voted is told so, never how. `POST /api/v1/poll` keeps, in D1 (`migrations/0008_poll.sql`), an HMAC of the poll and the network keyed with the Worker's `SESSION_SECRET`, and the choice: never the address. The front page's payload carries the counts (`poll`), and `/api/v1/status` says whether votes are counted (`poll.ready`); without the secret the poll hides itself. The static copy has none.

- Pepe reactions are kept for feed cards. Reactions sent before the cron first runs with this aren't there. No other cast text is kept, or even parsed.

- Editing the header search immediately hides suggestions for the previous words, and Escape also dismisses a lookup that is still loading. Arrow keys and Enter cannot open a result from an older query.
- An API answer with valid JSON but missing or invalid payload fields is requested again on the next visit. Malformed prefetched payloads are discarded instead of keeping the page in an error state for five minutes.
- The "Sound off · tap for sound" badge does what it says: it acts on the state it shows and changes its label at once, so a quick second tap no longer acts on a state the reader no longer sees.
- The Farcaster app is no longer called @warpcast anywhere: the "via" tooltip says "requested by Farcaster (app fid 9152)", and the "via Farcaster" links go to the app's account by fid instead of its old @warpcast name.
- On post pages, the likes, recasts and replies popovers no longer show under the replies or under a native tooltip: the post lifts above the replies while a popover is open, and the counts row no longer carries a `title` tooltip (its ranking note is read to screen readers instead).
- DEGEN staking promotions are left out as spam, like DEGEN reward bait: casts linking to or naming yeetr.fun, and "stake your DEGEN" or "yield on your DEGEN" pitches. Casts that just discuss staking stay.
- "new" on the front page lists the newest casts on Farcaster, seconds to a minute old. It used to re-sort the trending pool, whose newest cast was hours old: Hypersnap ranks trending casts with a lag by design (even its 1-hour window's casts are 1–2 hours old), and Hypersnap's search index was about 8 hours behind. "new" now comes from Snapchain's event stream (`/api/v1/latest`): each build reads only the events since the previous one, adds the new top-level casts and drops removed ones, and the list is rebuilt at the edge every 30 seconds. `/status` reports how old the newest cast in the new and trending lists is, and how old each list is.
- Muted text (times, handles, counts, the "read-only" pill) is easier to read: its color now has at least 4.5:1 contrast on cards and on the page background (it was 4.0:1 on cards and 3.7:1 on the background). Post pages have a level-one heading for screen readers ("Cast by …"), and the posting app's link in "Posted via" is underlined instead of told apart by color alone.
- The static test server answers malformed paths with 400 instead of crashing, and rejects paths into sibling directories whose names share its root prefix.
- Copying a verified wallet waits for the clipboard write before showing success, and reports failures with a retry instead of falsely saying “Copied”.
- Profiles preserve unknown vote states when the leave-vote source is unavailable or records disagree, and identify app-reported revocations without claiming they were confirmed onchain.
- Reaction dialogs keep keyboard focus inside, restore it when dismissed, and lock background scrolling. Their profile links use the app router, and navigating or going Back closes the dialog.
- Profiles say how an account voted in only three ways: "Voted yes" (stay), "Voted no" (leave) or "Didn't vote". A stay vote that Network School's moderation revoked counts as not voting, since revoking cancels it on Base. When the sources can't settle it, the vote is left out instead of saying "Unknown", and while it loads an empty pill replaces "checking…".
- Card text can be selected: dragging across it selects it instead of dragging a link, and doesn't open the post, while a plain click still does. The post's link for keyboards and screen readers is the timestamp.
- The YouTube and Vimeo click-to-load thumbnail no longer enlarges or brightens on hover. Media isn't highlighted or enlarged on hover anywhere.
- Clicking a card outside its links opened the post only after about 4 seconds. It used a view transition whose promise waited for an animation frame, which never comes during a transition, so Chrome's timeout ended it. Cards now open posts directly, in under 100 ms, with a short CSS fade-in.
- The docs and the stylesheet no longer mention or use view transitions, which cards stopped using in 0.3.x; the leftover cross-document `@view-transition` rule is gone too.
- The changelog's 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 entries link to their release tags (`v0.1.0`, `v0.2.0`) and compare views instead of bare commits. `scripts/check.sh` checks every release links to its tag.
- Revisiting a page after an API error (a timeout, a 500, "busy") asks the API again instead of showing the same error for up to 5 minutes. Prefetches no longer store errors, and a garbled answer is never reused as data.
- Back to the front page deep in the feed returns to the same card and stays there. Before, the list came back shorter (25 cards, without the later pages), and a position saved by pixels could not be reached or was moved by images loading, so it could jump to the top a few seconds later. The place is now saved as the card at the top of the viewport, lists come back as long as they were, and the place is held for 6 s unless the reader scrolls.
- Back to a feed no longer moves the page after the reader's place has been restored. Images above that place came back at zero height and grew as they loaded, pushing the page by hundreds of pixels at a time, sometimes after the 6 s hold had ended. Images seen before are now drawn at their size straight away.
- Going Back or navigating to another page dismisses the media viewer and releases its scroll lock, instead of leaving it over the destination page. Closing with Escape still returns focus to the image that opened it.
- A video about to autoplay no longer flashes the play icon over its poster: the icon stays hidden from the moment it is due to autoplay until it plays. It comes back if playback hasn't started within 2.5 s, for example when autoplay is refused.
- A video autoplaying muted no longer has the play button drawn over it, so it no longer looks paused. A small "Sound off · tap for sound" badge shows instead, and a click anywhere plays it with sound. Videos are muted and inline in their markup, and muted again before every autoplay, as iOS Safari requires.
- A video the reader started keeps playing when another video on the page could autoplay. Before, a muted autoplay that started at the same moment took over and stopped the reader's video.
- Autoplay goes to the topmost video in view. On phones, where a post's video and a reply's can both be on screen, whichever loaded last used to take over, so the reply's video could stop the post's. Once the playing video scrolls away, the next one in view starts.
- The Content Security Policy allows Cloudflare Web Analytics' script (`static.cloudflareinsights.com`). Cloudflare injects it when Web Analytics is on, and before, the browser blocked it on every page and logged an error.
- A cast whose posting app couldn't be looked up (Hypersnap failed, or the request's budget ran out) is looked up again on a later view. Before, it was remembered as having no app for as long as the Worker stayed warm.
- The header search box says "Search", so its placeholder no longer gets cut off on phones. Its accessible name still says what it searches.
- When the next page of the front page fails to load, it is tried again after 2, 4 and 8 seconds. Before, one failed page ended the feed for the rest of the visit.
- A short or empty filtered page no longer stops infinite scrolling while later casts remain. Lists recheck their end after a page arrives, respect retry backoff and the 1,000-cast request limit, and release their observer callbacks when unmounted. Ranked front-page batches are cached between reactive reads.
- Changing the front-page sort reorders each loaded trending page, including later pages, while keeping page boundaries stable as more arrives. Later pages retain their posting-app metadata, including when returning to the feed. A request that finishes after navigating away no longer accesses disposed feed state.
- Popovers (who liked, recasted, replies) open toward the side with more room, so the replies preview on the first card no longer slides under the sticky header. Their height fits the space available, and the card with an open popover sits above the next card instead of under it.
- Popovers (who liked, recasted, replied, and the Wallets list) have a solid background. In Chrome, a photo in the card above could show through them: their see-through, blurred background was drawn beneath images on their own compositing layer.
- Timestamps with impossible dates (30 February, 31 April), out-of-range time zone offsets or trailing text are rejected instead of being rolled over into a different date.
- Channel parent URLs and search terms retain literal percent escapes instead of being decoded again. The static API parser treats form-encoded spaces and literal plus signs the same way as the Worker.
- On touch screens, tapping a likes, recasts or replies count opens its popover. Before, the tap's focus opened it and the tap itself closed it again. On narrow screens, popovers are nudged to stay on screen.
- Escape closes a keyboard popover without reopening it on the returning focus. Preview links stay in the app, and leaving an open preview removes its outside-click listener.
- On phones, a cast with a wide image no longer makes its list wider than the screen.
- A cast whose channel URL holds a non-ASCII character where an address would start no longer crashes the page that lists it.
- On phones, profiles show the wallet activity panel and its Wallets button right after the profile header, instead of below every cast. The Wallets popover opens toward the side with more room, like the others.
- Profiles open by any name the account holds. `/u/dwr.eth` said "No such user": Hypersnap looks names up by each account's current fname only, and fid 3 is now `dwr`. Other names, ENS names among them, are now looked up by the hub's username proof, which names the account's FID.
- Casts that embed mini apps hosted on their own domains (for example a `*.vercel.app` app) are left out of every feed, search and reply list, like those on Farcaster's hosts. Hypersnap gives no embed metadata, so the Worker reads the linked page's `fc:miniapp`, `fc:frame` or `of:` meta tags: a few links per build, with each verdict remembered per URL.
- Clicking play on a video while its playlist is still loading plays it, with sound, once it has loaded. Before, the click was lost and the video stayed still.
- Leaving a loading video no longer lets a late playlist or autoplay timeout access disposed state and panic the app. Autoplay timers are cleared when replaced or when the video is removed.
## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added

- **Search**: one box in the header for casts, people and channels, with results grouped. Search hits are re-read in bulk so they carry their media and counts.
- **Who liked, recasted and replied**:
  - Hovering (or focusing) a likes or recasts count shows the ten newest people. Clicking opens the full list (up to 100, as many as Hypersnap returns).
  - Hovering a replies count shows a scrollable preview of the most engaged replies, with avatars, scores, text and image thumbnails, and a link to the full thread. On touch screens a tap opens it.
- **Wallets popover** on profiles: every verified EVM and Solana address, each with one-click copy and a "Copied" confirmation.
- **The Farcaster vote** on profiles: whether the account voted to stay or to leave when farcaster.xyz asked, or did not vote.
  - Stay votes are read onchain on Base, from Network School's opt-in NFT keyed by FID.
  - Leave votes come from farcaster.xyz's public API, since they are not recorded onchain.
  - See [docs/VOTE.md](docs/VOTE.md).
- **Instant navigation** ([docs/PERFORMANCE.md](docs/PERFORMANCE.md)):
  - A post opens from the cast data already in the feed and loads its replies behind it.
  - Hovering or touching a card prefetches its post, and cards that stay in view are prefetched in idle time, within a capped budget.
  - Infinite scroll loads the next trending page before the reader reaches the end.
  - Navigation uses View Transitions and Speculation Rules, with preconnects to media hosts.
  - Back and forward restore the scroll position.
  - CI checks a performance budget against the live site.
- **Videos autoplay muted when scrolled into view** and pause when scrolled away, one at a time. They never autoplay with reduced motion or Save-Data.
- The whole post card is clickable, while text stays selectable and links inside it still work.
- **Release process**: Semantic Versioning from the workspace `Cargo.toml`, Conventional Commits, this changelog, tagged GitHub releases, and short-lived branches merged by squash ([docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md)).
- The version and short commit appear in the footer, and the Worker answers `/version` (also `/api/v1/version`).
- A test of Neynar scores for known FIDs, and [docs/SCORE.md](https://github.com/ael-dev3/Allthing/blob/v0.3.0/docs/SCORE.md) on why the score is read onchain.

### Changed

- **Casts that embed mini apps or frames are filtered out server-side**: from the front page, channels, profiles, search and reply previews. They are detected by URL (`farcaster.xyz/miniapps/…`, `farcaster.xyz/~/mini-apps…`, `warpcast.com/~/frames…`, `*.neynar.app`, including links in the text), or by `fc:frame`/`fc:miniapp` metadata where an API provides it. In threads, a mini app reply is dropped, unless it has replies of its own; then it stays as "Mini app post hidden" so the thread holds together.
- Profiles keep only the wallet activity infographic in the side area.
- The header has Home, Channels and search; the footer is one line (license, source, version).
- Changing the sort reorders the list in place instead of rebuilding the page, and expanding replies keeps the scroll position.
- Neynar scores are cached for 12 hours instead of 6 (they change weekly at most).
- GitHub Pages now redirects to https://allthing.social, keeping paths and queries.

### Removed

- The "Hypersnap" backend badges on casts, the "Read from" and ingress lines of the post's provenance box, and "view on farcaster.xyz" links.
- The keyboard shortcuts box and the j/k/o/x bindings, with their selection highlight. The media viewer keeps its arrow keys and Escape.
- The About page, the intro box, "How ranking works", "Where data comes from", and the "Front page" heading.
- Hover enlargement of images.
- On profiles: the verified addresses list, the "Posts via" breakdown, the per-chain "no activity" notes, the Solana note and "How this is measured".

### Fixed

- Clicking play on a video that was autoplaying muted could leave it muted.
- Search queries with characters that Hypersnap's query parser treats as syntax (`:`, quotes, brackets) no longer fail.
